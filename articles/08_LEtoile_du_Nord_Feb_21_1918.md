# A Disastrous Fire

**Source:** *L'Étoile du Nord*, Joliette, Thursday, February 21, 1918, 34th Year, No. 31

---

After the Académie Saint-Viateur, fire has struck the Grey Nuns' convent in Montreal and reduced to ashes the greater part of this immense building. The damages are estimated, it is said, at more than one hundred thousand dollars, and approximately sixty children lost their lives amid the flames.

The newspapers report that the soldiers showed prodigious courage and zeal in this circumstance. Together with the nuns, whose magnanimity equals their devotion, they worked at rescuing the elderly, the invalids, and the children threatened by the fire.

When the fire broke out, there were in the rooms of the Crèche and the Orphanage, 170 children, 20 nuns, 20 nurse-attendants, and additionally in the establishment 85 infirm elderly men and 105 elderly women.

The military hospital — the part of the establishment reserved for soldiers, which was destroyed — contained 285 wounded soldiers.
