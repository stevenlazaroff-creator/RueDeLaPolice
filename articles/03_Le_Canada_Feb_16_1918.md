# The Governor General of Canada Addresses a Telegram of Sympathy to Monseigneur Bruchési

**Source:** *Le Canada*, Montreal, Saturday, February 16, 1918, Vol. XV, No. 267, Page 7

---

## The Latest Report Shows 53 Dead

Among the many expressions of sympathy received by the Reverend Grey Nuns regarding the catastrophe that so painfully struck them, is the following telegram, received from Their Excellencies the Governor General of Canada and the Duchess of Devonshire:

> To His Grace Monseigneur Paul Bruchési, Archbishop of Montreal:
>
> Their Excellencies have learned with regret of the loss of life in the fire at the Grey Nuns' Hospital and hope that the reports that have been published are exaggerated. They hope that you will offer the parents of these children their sincere sympathy.
>
> Colonel Henderson,
> Secretary to the Governor General,
> Ottawa.

To this telegram, His Grace the Archbishop responded:

> With the Grey Nuns so painfully tried, I thank Your Excellency for your kind sympathies. The disaster was great. Numerous, alas, are the poor little children who perished. But our nuns, admirably resigned, remain courageous in their misfortune. Soon, I hope, the wing of the Hospital intended for abandoned children will be rebuilt, and part of which had become the home of sick or wounded soldiers.
>
> The Archbishop of Montreal.

### The Right of Suffrage Will Be Granted to Women

*[Editor's note: The same page also contained a lengthy article about women's suffrage and an inventory of the national workforce, unrelated to the fire.]*

As we suggested in our report yesterday, the loss of life reached a high figure. The latest report from the morgue last night shows 53 deaths; this hecatomb will leave a painful memory among the venerable Grey Nuns.
